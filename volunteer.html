Why volunteer with us?

Meaningful, hands-on experience across a variety of programmes — pick what you care about.

🩸

Blood donation camps

Help coordinate blood donation drives — assist with registrations, donor care, and post-donation refreshments.

🏥

Health camps

Support health checkup and cataract operation camps, helping patients access free medical services.

🌱

Environment drives

Participate in plantation drives and pollution prevention initiatives for a greener Nagpur.

🤝

Social outreach

Help with clothes donation drives, old age home visits, and disaster relief food kit distribution.

What to expect

📧 We'll reach out

Our team will contact you within 3–5 business days via email or phone.
