Facts about blood donation

Every donation tells a story. Here's why it matters more than most people realise.

🩸
1 in 3

People will need a blood transfusion at some point in their lifetime — making each donation more vital than you think.

⏱️
30 min

Is all it takes to donate — and your blood can last up to 42 days.

👥
3 lives

Can be saved from a single donation, as blood is separated into components.

🔄
56 days

Is how long you wait between whole blood donations — completely safe.

❤️
O–

Is the universal donor blood type, compatible with all patients in emergencies.
